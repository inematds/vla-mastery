What are VLA models?

Vision-Language-Action models combine visual perception, natural language understanding, and motor action generation in a single neural network. They allow robots to interpret natural language instructions, observe the environment through a camera, and perform physical manipulations -- all end-to-end.

Who is this course for?

ML/AI engineers looking to move into robotics, robotics engineers looking to adopt foundation models, researchers following the VLA frontier, and product/strategy professionals who need to understand this technology’s impact over the next 5 years.
