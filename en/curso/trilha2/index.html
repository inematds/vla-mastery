MODULE 2.1

🐍 Python + PyTorch: The Dominant Stack for VLA

What it is: eager execution, autograd, and the HF ecosystem.

Why it matters: rapid iteration on architectures that change every week.

Concepts: define-by-run, CUDA/MPS, portability.

What it is: bf16/fp16 and 7B memory budget.

Why it matters: defines which GPU trains the model.

Concepts: autocast, Adam states, activations.

What it is: load VLMs and train QLoRA adapters.

Why it matters: fine-tune a 7B model on 24 GB.

Concepts: PEFT, NF4, target_modules.

What it is: Dataset/DataLoader and action normalization.

Why it matters: incorrect normalization breaks the action head.

Concepts: stats with ckpt, augmentation, workers.

What it is: state sharding across GPUs.

Why it matters: makes training large VLAs feasible.

Concepts: all-gather, reduce-scatter, recompute.

What it is: torch.compile and action chunking.

Why it matters: latency closes the control loop.

Concepts: inference_mode, CUDA graphs, export.

Concepts: eval rollout, success rate, closed loop.

What it is: decentralized middleware over DDS.

Why it matters: real time and safety.

Concepts: nodes, topics, services, actions.

What it is: perception, planning, and control as nodes.

Why it matters: swap the policy without changing the driver.

Concepts: rqt_graph, topic hz, modularity.

Why it matters: observations from the same instant; correct QoS.

Why it matters: safe approach and trajectory execution.

Concepts: ros2_control, e-stop, time parameterization.
